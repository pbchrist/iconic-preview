How to use this desk

Every morning: choose the newest fleet list and the latest two vAuto exports. Set the snapshot date to the date of the exports. Run the screen, review priority candidates and export the CSV. A HOLD means insufficient evidence or an unresolved warning, not that the vehicle is bad.

How ranking works: the engine normalizes supplier abbreviations, excludes current VINs from historical exits, matches year/make/model and trim within the mileage band, counts recent exits and current stock, then compares the supplier ask with the lowest comparable historical asking price minus your cost allowance and target margin. At most one priority unit per model family is selected.

Limitations: prototype only. Historical exports lack reliable sales confirmation; mileage of at least 5,000 is used as a used-like proxy. Trim matching is heuristic. Regional sales, actual gross, title, inbound units, transport and arbitration deadlines are not verified. Defaults are provisional, not dealer policy. No automatic purchasing. Use authorized exports only.

Privacy: no dealership records are embedded in the site. Processing occurs in browser memory. PDF and spreadsheet parsing libraries load from public CDNs; use only if approved by your organization. Reloading clears results.
