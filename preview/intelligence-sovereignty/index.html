01 / AN ARGUMENTTHE PRICE OF DEPENDENCE

They can change the machine. You've built your life around it.

AI is becoming the operating layer beneath work, commerce, research and ordinary thought. This should be an extraordinary expansion of human freedom.

Instead, much of it arrives as a subscription to somebody else's intelligence.

They choose the models. They change the limits. They keep the memory. They can take away the access.

None of this requires villains. You can believe in the technology and still refuse to hand its future to a small number of gatekeepers.

The question is simple: if an instrument becomes essential to your work, shouldn't you have the right to own and control it?
